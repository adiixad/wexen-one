If a separate written agreement covers your work, review it together with these terms.

01

Introduction

At Wexen, we respect your privacy and recognise the importance of protecting personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains how Wexen may collect, use, store, process, and protect information when you visit our website, submit an enquiry, communicate with us, or engage with our business.

Wexen aims to handle personal information responsibly, transparently, and only for legitimate business purposes.

By using our website or voluntarily providing information to us, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy, subject to any consent required under applicable law.

02

Information We May Collect

Depending on how you interact with Wexen, we may collect the following categories of information.

Personal Information

When you submit our contact form or communicate with us, we may collect:

  • Full name.
  • Email address.
  • Phone number, if provided.
  • Message or enquiry details.
  • Other information you voluntarily share with us.

Technical Information

When you visit our website, certain technical information may be collected automatically by the website, hosting provider, or related technical services. This may include:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) address.
  • Browser type and version.
  • Device and operating system information.
  • Pages visited and approximate visit times.
  • Referring website or source.
  • Basic website usage and diagnostic information.

The specific information collected depends on the website configuration and the providers used.

Business Communication Information

If you enter into discussions or a business engagement with Wexen, we may also process relevant communication records, project information, documents, approvals, and business contact details needed to manage that relationship.

Please avoid submitting passwords, payment card details, sensitive personal information, or confidential third-party information through a general website contact form.

03

How We Use Your Information

Wexen may use collected information for the following purposes:

  • To receive and respond to enquiries.
  • To understand your requirements and communicate with you.
  • To assess potential business engagements.
  • To prepare proposals, quotations, or relevant documentation.
  • To manage client communication and agreed work.
  • To maintain website functionality and performance.
  • To identify technical errors, suspicious activity, or misuse.
  • To protect our website, business, and users.
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations.
  • To maintain relevant business records.

We will not use personal information for unrelated purposes without an appropriate lawful basis or required consent.

04

Contact Form and Information Submission

When you submit the Wexen contact form, the information you provide may be transmitted to Wexen for the purpose of responding to your enquiry.

The form may collect your name, email address, optional phone number, and message.

If the website contact form is configured to use Google Apps Script and Google Sheets, submitted information may be processed through those services and stored in the associated business spreadsheet.

Please note that the actual storage and processing arrangement depends on the live website configuration.

05

Legal Basis and Consent

Wexen will process personal information only where there is an appropriate legal basis under applicable law.

Depending on the circumstances, this may include:

  • Your consent.
  • Responding to a request you have made.
  • Taking steps connected with a proposed or existing business engagement.
  • Meeting legal obligations.
  • Other lawful purposes permitted by applicable law.

Where consent is required, we will seek it in an appropriate manner. Where applicable, you may withdraw consent by contacting us. Withdrawal does not affect processing that was lawful before the withdrawal or processing supported by another lawful basis.

06

Sharing and Disclosure of Information

Wexen does not sell personal information to third parties.

We may share relevant information where reasonably necessary with:

  • Website hosting and infrastructure providers.
  • Email and communication providers.
  • Form-processing and data-storage providers.
  • Google services, including Google Sheets and Google Apps Script, if used for contact-form processing.
  • Professional advisers, auditors, or legal representatives.
  • Government authorities, regulators, or law-enforcement agencies where legally required.
  • Other parties where you have authorised the disclosure or applicable law permits it.

We aim to limit shared information to what is reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose.

Third-party providers may process information under their own privacy policies and contractual arrangements.

07

Data Storage and Retention

Wexen retains personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including responding to enquiries, managing business relationships, maintaining records, resolving disputes, and meeting legal obligations.

Retention periods may vary depending on the nature of the information and applicable requirements.

When information is no longer required, Wexen will take reasonable steps to delete, anonymise, or securely dispose of it, subject to lawful retention obligations and technical limitations.

08

Data Security

Wexen takes reasonable technical and organisational measures intended to protect personal information against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, loss, or misuse.

Depending on the systems used, these measures may include:

  • Restricted access to business information.
  • Secure account and credential management.
  • Appropriate access controls.
  • Use of reputable hosting and infrastructure providers.
  • Reasonable precautions when sharing documents.
  • Review and maintenance of website security.

No website, online transmission, or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Wexen therefore cannot promise absolute security, but aims to handle information with appropriate care.

09

Cookies and Similar Technologies

The Wexen website may use cookies or similar technologies where required for website functionality, security, analytics, or user experience.

Cookies may be used by the website itself or by third-party services integrated into it.

Where required, visitors will be provided with appropriate information and choices regarding non-essential cookies.

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Some website features may not function properly if certain cookies are disabled.

10

International Data Processing

Some third-party service providers used by Wexen may store or process information on servers located outside India.

Where personal information is transferred or processed internationally, Wexen will take reasonable steps to ensure that such processing is handled in accordance with applicable legal requirements and relevant contractual safeguards.

11

Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have rights relating to your personal information, which may include:

  • Requesting information about personal data processed by Wexen.
  • Requesting correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Requesting deletion of personal information where legally applicable.
  • Withdrawing consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Raising a privacy concern or grievance.
  • Seeking available remedies under applicable law.

To make a privacy-related request, contact us using the details provided below. We may request reasonable information to verify your identity before responding.

Certain information may need to be retained where required by law or where another lawful basis applies.

12

Children’s Privacy

The Wexen website is intended for general business and professional audiences and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children in circumstances where parental consent or other legal requirements apply.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to Wexen inappropriately, please contact us so that the matter can be reviewed and appropriate action can be taken.

13

Third-Party Websites

Our website may contain links to external websites, platforms, or services.

Wexen is not responsible for the privacy practices, security, content, or policies of third-party websites.

We recommend reviewing the privacy policy of any external website before sharing personal information with it.

14

Data Breach and Security Incidents

If Wexen becomes aware of a personal data breach affecting information under its control, it will assess the incident and take reasonable steps to contain, investigate, and address it.

Where notification is required, Wexen will provide the necessary information to affected individuals and relevant authorities in accordance with applicable law and prescribed requirements.

15

Changes to This Privacy Policy

Wexen may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in its website, business operations, technology, or applicable legal requirements.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

We encourage visitors and clients to review this page periodically.
