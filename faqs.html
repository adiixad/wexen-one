THE PRACTICAL DETAILS

Useful answers before you get started.

Every request is different. The details are discussed with you before any work is agreed.

01 What is Wexen?

Wexen is a flexible business-support partner that helps individuals, entrepreneurs, startups, and organisations manage business requirements, reduce repetitive work, and improve the way they operate. We focus on understanding your needs and finding a suitable way to support your goals.

02 What kind of work does Wexen handle?

Wexen does not follow a fixed list of services or predefined packages. We approach each requirement individually and discuss the most suitable way to support your business, project, or ongoing operations.

03 Who can work with Wexen?

Wexen works with startups, small businesses, entrepreneurs, freelancers, independent professionals, creators, and growing organisations. Whether you are managing a new idea or an established business, we are open to understanding your requirements.

04 How do I get started with Wexen?

Getting started is simple. Contact us through the website form or email us with a brief explanation of what you need. We will review your enquiry, understand your expectations, and discuss the possible next steps.

05 Does Wexen offer customised arrangements?

Yes. We understand that every business has different priorities, challenges, and working requirements. Our approach is flexible, and the scope, workflow, timelines, and commercial arrangements can be discussed according to the nature of the engagement.

06 How does Wexen manage projects and communication?

We begin by understanding the requirements, agreeing on the scope, and establishing the working arrangement. Communication, progress updates, feedback, and approvals are handled according to the agreed workflow to maintain clarity throughout the engagement.

07 Can I work with Wexen on a long-term basis?

Yes. Depending on your requirements and mutual agreement, Wexen can discuss both individual projects and ongoing business-support arrangements. The duration and working structure will be agreed upon before the engagement begins.

08 Does Wexen work with international clients?

Wexen can discuss enquiries from clients in different countries. Communication, project scope, payment arrangements, applicable taxes, timelines, and other relevant conditions will be discussed and agreed upon before starting work.

09 How does Wexen protect my business information?

We recognise the importance of privacy and confidentiality. Information shared during an enquiry or engagement is intended to be handled responsibly and used for relevant business purposes. Additional confidentiality arrangements can also be discussed when required. Please refer to our Privacy Policy for further information.

10 How can I discuss my requirements or request a quotation?

You can contact Wexen through the website contact form or email contact@wexen.org. Share your requirements and relevant details, and we will review your enquiry. Any proposed scope, quotation, timeline, and working arrangement will be discussed before proceeding.
